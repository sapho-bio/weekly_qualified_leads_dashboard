Data notes

  • Live HubSpot feed: rows marked "HubSpot (live)" come from HubSpot each Monday. A contact counts as Qualified when its lifecycle stage is Qualified Lead, Scientist Review, Building Quote, Quote Issued, Account Created or Active Client, dated by the day it entered that stage. A new web-form contact still at the Lead stage appears as New inbound. Cold imported contacts are left out. The rules live in config.json.
  • Periods: reporting periods run in three-month blocks starting Aug 1, 2026 (Aug to Oct, Nov to Jan, and so on). The newest block opens automatically.
  • Undated X leads: Regenerative Research and Peptide Critic have no date in the X DM slide. Dates marked with a tilde for the other X leads come from the day their first Lab/Sales ticket was logged.
  • Names not recorded: READY, Rebody Health, Thaeta Scientific, Curexa and the Bordeaux's pharmacy contacts have no full contact name in the notes.
  • Source calls: Inbound forms with a Google, Bing or DuckDuckGo referrer are Website organic. Metro Meds had no referrer, so it is also Website organic. The Sep 9 Dunivant lead is Website ad because Pavel confirmed it in the Slack thread.
  • Existing accounts: The Lab/Sales ticket DB also lists Striker, Art of Medicine, Sweetgrass, C3, Align, Hillsdale, EmeraldsRx, Canada, Hillstone, Creative Compounding, Spring Creek, Cache Valley, Gibsons, The Pharmacy Place. They look like existing clients, so they are not counted as new leads.
  • Business cards: All 12 scanned. Eleven matched existing contacts and now carry the card details (title, phone, email, address, website, handwritten notes), marked with a Card tag. Raymond L. Solano III (PDLabs) had no entry of his own, only a mention in Michael Macione's note, so he sits under Card, status unknown.
  • HubSpot check (Oct 5): every lead was searched by company name, domain and email. Matches on domain only are noted. Possible matches are flagged where the name fits but the location or domain does not. Three leads here are already active clients in HubSpot (ReviveRx, John's Rx, Pharmatron), and Medivera is at Quote Issued. The counts above still include them. Duplicate HubSpot records exist for Monica Quiros, Lily Niehaus and Wedgewood.
  • Fit and intent: these are separate ratings. Fit is how well the account matches our buyer: High is a sterile or multi-site pharmacy or large account, Medium is non-sterile, small, or an adjacent brand, Low is not a buyer (vendors, influencers, investors, consultants), Unknown means the type was not recorded. Intent is High when a need is stated and something is under way (call held, RFP, quote, ticket, active client), Medium when a need is stated with nothing under way, Low when the meeting was positive but no need was stated, and None for partners and intros.
  • Logged by: the person whose notes the lead comes from (Pavel's or Abraham's conference notes, Pavel's X and event slides). Web form leads come from the HubSpot ticker. The HubSpot owner is shown separately in each row.
  • Quiet period: No qualified leads were logged for the weeks of Sep 21 and Sep 28.
